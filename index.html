Transparent Peer Review

Expertise and school of thought, in the open

In formation
Transparent Peer Review is in formation. Reviewer profiles open soon.

Why

Knowledge is transparent. Review can be robust, verifiable and open about who is judging, whether or not the reviewer's name is on it: what a reviewer knows and where they stand are visible to everyone.

Science advances funeral by funeral: people are loath to challenge a prestigious scholar even when they have proof. When a reviewer's standpoint is on the record, a challenge can be weighed on its merits, and so can a dismissal.

How it works

  1. Build a reviewer profile.Choose your fields and subfields. Your profile shows what you know in them and where you stand on their contested questions. No credential is required; the profile is.
  2. Your standpoint is public.Authors and readers can walk through the profile behind any review, and compare it with their own.
  3. Your name is your choice.Sign your reviews or keep your name off the record, depending on the situation.
  4. Review what's relevant to you.Articles in your fields, published anywhere or submitted here. Reviewers build on and dispute each other's reviews, in the open.
  5. Readers rate the raters.Reviews and annotations are rated with Woxx ratings on several axes, never collapsed to one score, and everyone's ratings can be viewed side by side.

For journals

Not a yes/no gate: work goes up and is annotated in the open, by reviewers whose standpoint you can see. Transparent Peer Review is the review engine for Yavin's journals, and will be open to any journal that wants this process instead of the current one.
