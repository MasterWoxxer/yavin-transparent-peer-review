Transparent Peer Review

Expertise and school of thought, in the open

In formation
Transparent Peer Review is in formation. Reviewer sign-up is not open yet.

Why

Science advances funeral by funeral: people are loath to challenge a prestigious scholar even when there is proof. Here, anyone can show what they know and where they stand, then review in the open.

How it works

  1. Take the field quizzes.Pick your fields and subfields. The quizzes show your competence and your positions on the field's contested questions.
  2. Your results are public; your identity is protected.Anyone, including an author you review, can walk through your answers, or take the same quiz and compare theirs with yours.
  3. Review and annotate.Articles published anywhere, and work submitted here. Reviewers build on and dispute each other's reviews.
  4. Readers rate the raters.Reviews and annotations are rated on several axes, never collapsed to one score.

For journals

Not a yes/no gate: work goes up and is annotated in the open. Transparent Peer Review is the review engine for Yavin's journals, and will be open to any journal that wants this process instead of the current one.
